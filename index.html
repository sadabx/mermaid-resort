Our Story

Welcome to Mermaid Resort

A tranquil escape on Bangladesh's only coral island, where luxury meets nature's beauty

Your Island Paradise Awaits

Nestled along the pristine shores of Saint Martin's Island, Mermaid Resort offers an unparalleled tropical getaway. As the only coral island in Bangladesh, Saint Martin's provides a unique ecosystem of breathtaking beauty.

Our resort combines modern comfort with traditional Bangladeshi hospitality, creating a sanctuary where you can unwind to the rhythm of the waves. Wake up to spectacular ocean views, savor fresh seafood cuisine, and explore the vibrant marine life just steps from your door.

Whether you're seeking a romantic escape, family adventure, or peaceful retreat, our dedicated team ensures your stay is nothing short of magical.
